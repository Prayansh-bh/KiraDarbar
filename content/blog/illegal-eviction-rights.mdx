# Illegal Eviction Under The Model Tenancy Act

Being forced out of your housing without process is terrifying. Landlords often exploit the sheer speed of their actions to bypass judicial scrutiny entirely. 

However, under the **Model Tenancy Act 2021** currently cascading into local jurisdictions nationwide, physical eviction without extensive warning is an explicit crime.

## The Absolute Ban on "Self-Help" Evictions

A landlord cannot theoretically:
- Change your locks while you are working.
- Physically extract your personal belongings.
- Disconnect vital necessities strictly including Water and Electricity.

If your water or electricity has been cut, you can immediately seek a **civil injunction** against them demanding restoration under extreme punitive damages.

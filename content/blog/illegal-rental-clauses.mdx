# 5 Illegal Rental Agreement Clauses in India

Rent agreements are frequently drafted explicitly favoring the landlord. Sometimes, they include terms that are outright illegal. If you spot these, do not assume they bind you legally, courts generally strike them out instantly.

## 1. Absolute Liability for Normal Wear & Tear
If your agreement dictates you must return the flat in "exactly the pristine identical condition", that is impossible. You are explicitly legally protected against reasonable depreciation (faded paint, minor scuffs).

## 2. Immediate Eviction Without Notice
Any clause declaring "The landlord reserves the absolute right to evict within 5 hours" is void. Law explicitly mandates minimal statutory notice boundaries (often strictly 30 days minimum) regardless of extreme drafting.

## 3. The Blanket Non-Refundable Deposit
Under legal doctrine, Security Deposits are definitionally *refundable* hedges against proven damage. If a landlord drafts "₹1 Lakh Non-Refundable Entry Fee" pretending it's a deposit, this violates core exchange law.

## 4. Forced Undocumented Maintenance Spikes
If the agreement attempts to grant the landlord unilateral power to increase maintenance fees "as they deem fit" without structural explanation or HOA documentation, courts actively sever this clause.

## 5. Total Lockout of Guests
Reasonable enjoyment of your leased property includes visitors. Unless extreme noise restrictions are explicitly mandated, a clause universally preventing anyone else from ever existing in the property can be challenged.

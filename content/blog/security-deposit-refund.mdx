# Recovering Your Security Deposit in India

One of the most endemic problems renters face locally is landlords arbitrarily creating invalid justifications to withhold, deduct, and entirely steal their tenant's Security Deposits. 

If this has happened to you, understand strictly: **You are not helpless.** 

## Legal Framework Restrictions

Under modern tenancy architectures guided by the Model Tenancy Act, a landlord **cannot** legally withhold your deposit without documented, itemized, and proven proof of specific structural damages exceeding fair wear and tear.

  <strong>The 30 Day Rule:</strong> Most State Rent control acts prescribe that deposits must be refunded within exactly 30 days of standard vacancy or key-handover.
</div>

### Standard Steps to Secure Your Refund

1. **Invoke Formal Communication:** Stop using WhatsApp immediately. Move entirely to standardized email communications specifically CCing formal counsel if available.
2. **Execute a Walkthrough:** Never vacate without explicitly enforcing a signed sign-off form detailing exactly the condition of the apartment you are abandoning.
3. **Draft a Legal Notice:** If they refuse payment post 30 days, your absolute most powerful trump card is issuing a formalized Legal Notice signed by a licensed advocate demanding payment or threatening litigation via the Rent Authority.
